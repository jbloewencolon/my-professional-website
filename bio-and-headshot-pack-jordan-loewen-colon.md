## Long bio — ~290 words (press kit, full profile)

> Jordan Loewen-Colón, PhD, is a Responsible-AI strategist and ethics advisor whose work sits at the intersection of emerging technology, philosophy, and Indigenous data sovereignty. He writes as Taíno, and that vantage shapes a conviction at the center of his practice: that ethical technology is something organizations build deliberately, from the start — not a constraint bolted on after a system is already loose in the world.
>
> He is cofounder of the AI Alt Lab, which partners with state and local governments — including Utah and the City of Berkeley — as well as AI startups and large health organizations, helping them align data practices with ethical principles and prepare their governance structures for generative AI. He is also the founder of Supernova Immersives, a digital-therapeutics startup.
>
> A former AI, Ethics, and Data Justice Fellow at Queen's University's Centre for Health Innovation, Jordan now teaches at Queen's and lectures at Syracuse University. His commentary and research appear in the *Harvard Business Review* and in peer-reviewed journals spanning AI ethics, Indigenous data governance, and the philosophy of technology; he is also affiliated with the Aspen Policy Academy and FASPE. His forthcoming book, *Reality Technologies: The Promises and Problems of Tech Culture* (Fortress Press), examines how today's technologies reshape how we think, feel, and live.
>
> He holds a PhD from Syracuse University, a Master of Divinity from Princeton Theological Seminary, and a BA from The Ohio State University. Based in Toronto, he speaks internationally and reaches broad audiences through podcasts and live streams.
>
> Jordan is available for keynotes, workshops, advisory seats, and select consulting engagements.
